As for memory safety there aren't really any benefits for rust over golang since both have a garbage collector. The main thing I care about is desigining for a process where we encounter the fewest amount of bugs, and can guarentee as many things ahead of time work. - Technical Exellence and Reliability (Our users want plug and play software, that replaces the large tech giants at a similar level of reliability with your own hardware, they also want this without having to pay 1000 dollars up front for a fancy computer, importantly, I think this means that our users have a much higher standard of reliability because instead of going (oh I just need to change this 1 line in a config file, they will just say, oh its broken, then return it and never buy from us again) all of this means ensuring that we have a really really high level of reliability, and its so so so much harder to design for that later.
